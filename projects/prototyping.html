The Bottle Project: Solving for Threads

One of my recent challenges is designing a screw-on lid with specific tolerances to ensure a tight seal. I am using SolidWorks to iterate through different thread profiles.


Initial Prototype Test

Focus was on basic thread engagement. Created a cylinder with taps on both side. One side used the Hole wizard feature and has 118º point. Other is done simpler with flat ended die. Both die have a diameter of 2.48in and both taps have a diameter of 2.53. Waiting to be able to print to test fitment.

Version 1

Transformed from the test prototype. Hollowed out the center of the die to be the container. Cut the cylinder in half and focused on one side. Thinned out the cylinder walls to line up with the outside diameter of the die. Ended up sanding it up to 2500 grit to get smooth finish.

Version 2

Changed the design from V1 because there were too many threads. Kept some of the same measurements except the tread height on the container (die) and the lid (tap) height.

Version 3

Fine tuned the container from V2. Made the diameter of the inner circle on the container larger for greater volume and thinner walls. Did the same for the lid with 0.05in difference from the container for tolerance. Increased the radius of the filets on the top and bottom by 0.1 to make it rounder.
